Welcome to the GIS Student Council Election.

What is the Student Council?

A team of students chosen by you. They listen to your ideas, plan events, and work with teachers to make our school better for everyone.

Our goals

Give every student a voice. Plan fun events for the whole school. Keep GIS kind, safe and green. Help our community, like last year’s clean-up drives and Children’s Day outreach.

Your rights today

Every child has the right to be heard. That is a human right, written in the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child, Article 12. You are free to choose any candidate. Your vote is secret. You may also skip any vote. Nobody is allowed to tell you who to pick.
